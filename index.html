Villa Arvedi

37023 Grezzana, Verona  ·  villarvedi.it ↗

One of the most majestic Venetian villas in the Veneto region, Villa Arvedi stands in dominant position over the Valpantena valley, a few kilometers from Verona. Originally built in the 15th century and renovated in 1656 by architect Giovanni Battista Bianchi, the villa has been in the Arvedi family since 1824.

Its grand salons — the Sala dei Titani and Sala dei Cesari — are decorated with original frescoes, oil portraits, and marble floors, while the celebrated Giardino all'Italiana unfolds below the baroque terrace. The estate featured as a film location in Letters to Juliet.

I.

How We Met

In 2021, during the height of COVID shutdowns, fate brought Amaziah and Santana to Burn at The Battery Atlanta.

Amaziah was simply looking to get out of the house and enjoy basketball replays at a lowkey spot in the area. Meanwhile, Santana and her cousin Ashley were exploring the newly opened businesses at The Battery for an upcoming "Cousin Day" for their family. After hopping from place to place, their final stop was Burn, where they met Amaziah.

When Santana and Ashley arrived, there were two customers inside: Amaziah and another person. Santana and Ashley joined Amaziah and talked to him for an hour. Santana and Amaziah exchanged numbers, started dating and the rest is history!

"Ashley and I left Burn and walked to the car, then I realized I forgot my debit card. I went back to get it and Amaziah was still there. He asked if I had a business card so that he could get my number LOL."

Are any documents and/or processes needed to enter Italy?

Yes, you will need your passport and EES. It's not confirmed yet, but you may also need ETIAS.

EES (Entry/Exit System) requires each person to do facial recognition and fingerprint upon arriving in Europe. There is no cost for this, and it is mandatory. The process is completed at the airport. The information will be connected to your passport and will replace a passport stamp.

ETIAS (European Travel Information and Authorization System) is a mandatory, electronic, visa-waiver authorization projected to launch Q4 2026. The purpose is to prescreen travelers for security, public health, and migration risks before they arrive at the border. The cost is 20 euros and the application will be completed online.

Depending on when this launches, no one may need to fill this out. We will provide more information on EES and ETIAS as we get updates!

Travel Essentials

Pack smart for Italy.

1. Plug Adapter

Verona and Venice wall outlets do not mirror USA outlets. Type C and Type L adapters are needed. A universal adapter works across multiple countries.

2. Portable Charger

Keeps your phone charged throughout the day as you won't always be near wall outlets.

3. Compression Socks

Encourages blood flow and helps prevent swelling on long flights.

4. Snacks

Always a good idea to have your favorite snacks on the plane and in your room for last minute cravings!

5. Headphones

Noise cancelling headphones are helpful for a relaxing and quiet flight.

6. First Aid Kit

Helpful for minor accidents while traveling.

7. Medicine

Bring allergy meds, pain killers, supplements, vitamins, digestion aids, etc. just in case.

8. Neck Pillow

Creates comfort when sleeping on the plane.

9. Eye Mask

Helpful when sleeping on the plane.

10. Comfortable Shoes

There will be lots of walking on cobblestone streets — comfort is key!

11. Mini Umbrella

We all know weather can be unpredictable!

12. Crossbody Bag / Fanny Pack

Optimal choice for carrying personal items. Backpacks are not recommended for safety purposes.

13. AirTag

Track your bag if it inadvertently gets lost.

14. Travel Garment Steamer

Will help to quickly remove wrinkles in clothes after a long journey.

15. Electrolytes

Stay hydrated and energized during travel and long days of sightseeing.

16. Portable Fan

Helpful for warm afternoons while exploring the city.

17. Jacket

Evenings in Verona can be cool in October — layers are key.

18. Reusable Water Bottle

Stay hydrated on the go. Italy has many public fountains with fresh drinking water.

19. Euros

Italy uses Euros. Exchange currency before your trip or upon arrival at the airport.
